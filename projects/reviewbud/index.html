ReviewBud

January 10, 2025 · 1 min read

Streamline your business reviews with AI-powered review generation and QR code magic.

What it does

ReviewBud helps business owners create QR codes that customers can scan to leave AI-generated Google reviews. No more struggling to get customers to write reviews.

Tech stack

Django, Google OAuth, OpenAI API, Google Places API. Frontend is HTML/CSS with vanilla JavaScript. Hosted on Railway.
