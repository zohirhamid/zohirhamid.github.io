Project · 2025

ReviewBud

Django · OpenAI · Google APIs · Railway

ReviewBud streamlines business reviews with AI-assisted writing and simple QR codes.

What it does

Business owners create QR codes for customers to scan. ReviewBud then helps those customers put together a thoughtful Google review without starting from a blank page.

Tech stack

The product uses Django, Google OAuth, the OpenAI API, and the Google Places API. The frontend is HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, hosted on Railway.
