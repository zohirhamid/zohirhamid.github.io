JustDo

February 2, 2026 · 1 min read

A minimal, elegant todo app for people who want to organize tasks without the clutter.

What it does

JustDo lets you quickly add tasks, tag them by category (job hunting, leetcode, backend, etc.), and drag-and-drop to prioritize. Filter by tags, toggle dark/light mode, and stay focused on what matters.

Tech stack

Django REST Framework with JWT authentication on the backend. React with Vite on the frontend. SQLite for dev, PostgreSQL-ready for production.
